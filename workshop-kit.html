KOLLAB YOUTH · AN INDEPENDENT PRACTICE KIT

A small scene.
A useful next step.

Make one instruction or rule. Test what it communicates. Revise one thing. Keep a little evidence of your thinking.

No device is required. Paper and pencil are enough. Website use, speaking, partner work and sharing are optional. There is no score or timer. Use a language or story context you know; drawings and plain words count.

A short facilitator guide

Before a session, check device restrictions, downloads and printing. Use the activity link, the downloadable offline kit or this worksheet. No student account, AI account or subscription is required.

5 minutes

  1. 2 min: Choose a fictional scene. Keep or change one instruction.
  2. 2 min: Read it as a new player. Make one revision if useful.
  3. 1 min: Keep a next step. Sharing is optional.

15 minutes

  1. 2 min: Choose a comfortable way to participate.
  2. 4 min: Adapt a starter scene.
  3. 4 min: Begin one line, sketch or rule.
  4. 3 min: Test and revise.
  5. 2 min: Choose support and a next step.

30 minutes

  1. 3 min: Access, goals and optional sharing.
  2. 6 min: Make and try an opening.
  3. 8 min: Build a tiny paper prototype.
  4. 6 min: Observe and revise.
  5. 4 min: Connect a role or trade-off.
  6. 3 min: Keep evidence and next step.

Try a neutral question

“What do you think you can do?” “Where did you hesitate?” “What would you try next?” Observe the design, not whether the player is “good.” Solo alternative: test both paths and write what remains uncertain.

Respect choice and privacy

No name, camera, recording, family information or account is needed. Ask before showing anyone’s work. Passing, quiet observation and breaks count. Use fictional scenes, not personal stories. If a safety concern is raised, pause public discussion and follow your organization’s safeguarding/reporting procedures; do not promise secrecy.

This guide suggests activities. It does not arrange a mentor, placement, follow-up or admission. Completed clicks do not prove job readiness. Ask Kollab staff or a trusted adult what support is available.

My small scene experiment

Use words, another language, a sketch, or a mix. Leave private details out. You decide whether to share.

1. My first version

□ I used a supplied starter□ I changed a starter□ I made my own

2. What I or a tester noticed

What did the player think they could do? What was unclear? What is still uncertain?

3. One revision and my reason

I changed… because…

4. My next small step

□ Today□ This week□ When I have what I need□ Not sure yet

5. Support that would help

□ I can start□ Materials / a device□ Feedback□ A teammate□ Quiet / more time□ I will ask a trusted adult

Keep a before/after plus your explanation. An unfinished first version can still teach you something.
